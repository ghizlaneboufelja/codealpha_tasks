# CodeAlpha — Phishing Awareness Training

**Task 2 | Cyber Security Internship**

## Project overview
This training resource helps users recognize suspicious messages, identify fake websites, understand social-engineering tactics, and respond safely.

## Learning objectives
- Recognize warning signs in phishing emails and messages.
- Check website addresses and links more safely.
- Understand manipulation tactics such as urgency, authority, fear, and curiosity.
- Apply practical protection steps and report suspicious messages.
- Test understanding with a short quiz.

## Project contents
- `presentation/Phishing_Awareness_Training.pptx` — training slides and quiz.
- `resources/quiz-answer-key.md` — answers and explanations.
- `resources/participant-checklist.md` — quick-reference checklist.

## How to use
1. Present slides 1–8 as the training.
2. Ask participants to answer the quiz on slide 9.
3. Reveal slide 10 and discuss the explanations.
4. Share the checklist as a takeaway.

## Safety note
The email shown in the presentation is fictional and uses the reserved `.invalid` domain. This material is for awareness and education. Do not test phishing on real people without explicit authorization.
