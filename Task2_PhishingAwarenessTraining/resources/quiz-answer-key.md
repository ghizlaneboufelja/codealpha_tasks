# Quiz Answer Key

1. **Do not use the link.** Open the official app or type the known website address yourself.
2. **Verify independently.** Contact the manager through a known phone number or established channel before paying.
3. **No.** HTTPS encrypts traffic, but a phishing website can also use HTTPS.
4. **Report immediately.** Contact IT/security or the service provider. Change the exposed password from a trusted device, and change it anywhere else it was reused.
5. **Use a separate trusted channel.** Do not reply to the suspicious message or use its contact details.

## Scoring suggestion
- 1 point per correct answer.
- 4–5: Excellent awareness.
- 3: Review the warning signs and checklist.
- 0–2: Revisit the training and try again.
