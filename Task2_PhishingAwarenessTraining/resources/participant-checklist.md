# Phishing Awareness — Quick Checklist

Before you click or respond:
- [ ] Was I expecting this message?
- [ ] Does the full sender address make sense?
- [ ] Is the message creating urgency, fear, or pressure?
- [ ] Is it asking for a password, verification code, payment, or sensitive data?
- [ ] Can I verify the request through a separate trusted channel?
- [ ] Can I visit the official website or app directly instead of using the link?
- [ ] If suspicious, have I reported it?

If you clicked or shared information:
1. Report it promptly to the relevant IT/security team or service provider.
2. Change any exposed password from a trusted device; update other accounts if the password was reused.
3. Follow your organization's incident-response instructions.
